# Ridge install

First field deployment of a revision 3 station, on the north ridge at 2140 m.

## Conditions

Clear, 3 °C, wind from the west at 6 to 9 m/s. The gateway sits in the hut 1.8 km away with line of sight.

## Checklist

- [x] Mast guyed at three points
- [x] Panel tilted 55° facing south
- [x] First frame acknowledged at 11:42 UTC
- [ ] Return in spring to inspect the anemometer bearings

## Notes

Link margin was 14 dB at spreading factor 9, matching the estimate in [[Architecture]]. Temperature agreed with the reference thermometer within 0.1 °C after applying the offsets from [[Calibration]].

> [!NOTE]
> The [[Power Budget]] assumed 4 µA sleep current. The installed board measured 4.3 µA.
