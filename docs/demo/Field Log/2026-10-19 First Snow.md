# First snow

Twelve centimetres overnight. The station kept reporting.

## Observations

- Panel output dropped to 8 % of clear-sky for most of the day.
- Cell voltage fell 6 mV, well inside the [[Power Budget]].
- Wind readings froze at 0.0 m/s between 03:10 and 07:45 UTC. Rime on the cups is the likely cause.
